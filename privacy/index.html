K&P Pet Waste Removal — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective October 1, 2026

The short version: I collect what I need to clean your yard and bill you. I share it only with the services that make that possible. I never sell it. Your card number goes straight to Stripe, and I never see it.

1. Who I am

K&P Pet Waste Removal is a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Mahcia Davis in New Castle, Delaware ("K&P," "I," "me"). This policy covers this website (kpscoop.com) and the service I provide to customers.

2. What I collect

  • What you give me on the signup form: your name, service address, email, phone number, number of dogs, and yard size.
  • Your agreement: which version of the Service Agreement you accepted.
  • Payment details, handled by Stripe: your billing name, email, billing address, and card details. I can see that a payment was made, the amount, and limited details such as card brand and last four digits. I never see your full card number.
  • Service records: visit dates and times, how long a visit took, and notes about your yard's condition.
  • Photos: the photo of your closed gate that I take after each visit.
  • Messages: texts and emails you send me, and the phone number you text from.
  • Technical details from visiting this site: your IP address and browser information, logged automatically by the company that hosts the site (see Section 4).

3. How I use it

  • To confirm your yard, your area, and your price before service starts
  • To schedule and perform your service
  • To text you before I arrive, after I finish, and with your gate photo
  • To bill you and manage your plan
  • To answer your questions and handle problems
  • To keep the records I need for taxes, accounting, and insurance
  • To handle an insurance claim, if one ever arises from a visit
  • To meet legal requirements

5. What I don't do

  • I don't sell your personal information.
  • I don't share it for advertising.
  • I don't send marketing texts or emails. Messages from me are about your service.
  • I don't post, share, or publish photos of your property without your written permission.

6. Cookies, tracking, and Do Not Track

This site does not use cookies of its own, analytics, or advertising trackers. I don't track you across other websites, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off. I don't respond to those signals, because I'm not tracking.

One thing is outside my control. The payment button on this site loads code from Stripe. When you use it, Stripe may collect information about your device and browser, and may use cookies, to prevent fraud and run its service. So to answer directly: yes, another party, Stripe, may collect information about you when you use the payment button. Stripe's practices are described in its privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

7. How I protect your information

  • Card numbers never reach me. Stripe handles them under industry payment security standards.
  • Encrypted connections. This site and the signup form use HTTPS, so what you type travels encrypted.
  • Only I have access. I'm the only person at K&P, with no employees or subcontractors. Nobody else sees your information.
  • Password-protected accounts. The accounts where your information lives, including email, Stripe, and Formspree, are password-protected.
  • Photos stay with the service. Gate photos are used only to confirm your service.

No system is perfectly secure. If I learn of a breach that affects your information, I'll tell you promptly and as the law requires.

8. How long I keep it

I keep your information while you're a customer. After you leave, I keep billing and service records for as long as I need them for tax, accounting, insurance, and legal purposes, then delete them. I keep gate photos only as long as I need them to answer a question about a visit.

9. Your choices: see, correct, or delete

You can ask me for a copy of what I have about you, to correct anything that's wrong, or to delete it. Text or email me, and I'll respond within 30 days. I may need to keep some records after a deletion request, such as billing records for tax purposes, and I'll tell you what I'm keeping and why.

10. Children

My service is for property owners and adults. I don't knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If I make a material change, I'll update the effective date at the top of this page and tell current customers by text or email before the change takes effect. The current version always lives at kpscoop.com/privacy.
