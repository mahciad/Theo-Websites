K&P Pet Waste Removal — Service Agreement

Version 1.1 · Effective October 13, 2026

This agreement is between K&P Pet Waste Removal, a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Mahcia Davis in New Castle, Delaware ("K&P," "I," "me"), and the customer receiving service ("you").

By checking the box on the signup form or at checkout, you agree to these terms.

1. What I do

I remove dog waste from the accessible areas of your yard on a regular schedule, or once for a one-time cleaning. I walk the whole yard, not just the visible spots.

Service is for dog waste only.

2. Where and when

  • Service area: New Castle, Delaware (19720) and nearby neighborhoods.
  • Service day: Saturdays, between 8:00 AM and 4:00 PM.
  • Plans: weekly, or every other week.
  • I text you before I arrive and again when I'm done.

3. Pricing

Plan Monthly price
Weekly, 1 dog, under ¼ acre $95
Every other week, 1 dog, under ¼ acre $70
Each additional dog +$10
One-time cleaning $75 (see Section 4)

Yard size. These tiers apply to both the weekly and the every-other-week plan:

Yard size Added per month
Under ¼ acre base price
¼ acre to under ½ acre +$15/month
½ acre to 1 acre (1 included) +$30/month
Over 1 acre custom quote, text for a price

Yards with more than 4 dogs or over 1 acre are priced by custom quote.

Your price is confirmed after I review your yard size and number of dogs. If the yard turns out larger than described, or more dogs live there, I'll tell you the corrected price before your next billing date.

4. First visits and one-time cleanings

Recurring plans: there is no setup or first-visit fee. Your first visit includes up to 30 minutes of work. If the yard needs more, additional time is billed at $15 per 15 minutes. I'll tell you before going over.

One-time cleaning: $75, including up to 60 minutes of work. Additional time is billed at $15 per 15 minutes.

Very heavy first cleanups may be split across two visits so your trash bin isn't overloaded.

5. Billing and payment

  • Recurring plans are billed monthly, in advance, by card through Stripe.
  • Billing starts on your first service Saturday.
  • The monthly price is the same whether a month has four or five Saturdays. It holds your Saturday slot.
  • Declined payments: if a payment doesn't go through, I'll contact you to sort it out before your service date.

6. Your part

So I can do the job safely and on time:

  • Dogs must be indoors during service. Always. This is a safety rule, not a preference.
  • Gates must be unlocked and accessible on your service day.
  • The yard must be passable — no hazards that make walking it unsafe.

7. Locked gates

If I arrive and can't get in, the visit counts as completed and is billed — the slot was reserved and the trip was made.

If you text me to say the gate is unlocked, I'll make it up the same week when I can.

8. Weather

I work in most conditions. I reschedule only for weather that makes service unsafe, and I'll tell you in advance.

9. Holidays

If your service Saturday falls on a major holiday, that week is skipped. Service resumes the following Saturday at no extra charge, even though the yard will need a little extra time.

10. Pausing service

  • You can pause anytime. There's no fee.
  • Pauses run in whole billing months. Text me at least 3 days before your billing date and that month isn't charged.
  • For shorter trips, your rate stays the same — it holds your Saturday slot.
  • Your first visit after a pause follows first-visit terms: 30 minutes included, then $15 per 15 minutes if needed.

11. Cancelling

  • No contract. Cancel anytime, with no fee.
  • Text me at least 3 days before your billing date.
  • Service continues through the end of the month you've already paid for. There are no partial refunds.

12. If I miss a visit

If I miss a scheduled visit for any reason on my end, that visit is credited on your next bill.

This doesn't apply to visits skipped under Section 7 (locked gates), Section 8 (unsafe weather), or Section 9 (holidays).

13. Waste disposal

Waste is double-bagged and placed in your own trash bin for regular collection.

14. Gate photos and your privacy

I send you a photo of your closed, latched gate after every visit. Photos of your property are used only to confirm service. I never post, share, or publish them without your written permission.

15. Health and sanitation

I disinfect my tools and boots before entering each yard, so nothing travels from someone else's dogs to yours.

16. Price changes

Rates are reviewed annually. I'll give you at least 30 days' notice by text before any price change takes effect. If you don't want to continue at the new price, you can cancel under Section 11 before it applies.

17. Insurance and liability

K&P carries general liability insurance through Pet Care Insurance. A certificate of insurance is available on request.

If your dog is accidentally injured during a visit. My policy includes veterinary expense reimbursement for accidental injury to a domestic animal that occurs while in my care during service. Tell me as soon as you can so it can be reported in time, and keep your veterinary records and receipts. The insurer may ask to have the animal examined by a veterinarian of their choosing, at their expense.

If I damage something on your property. Tell me within 48 hours of the visit, with a description and photos. I'll review it with you directly. Where my insurance applies, I'll submit it; where it doesn't, I'll work it out with you myself. Either way, you hear from me — you won't be handed a policy number and left to chase it.

Report incidents promptly. My policy only responds to incidents reported within its reporting window. Late notice can leave a claim uncovered, so please raise anything the same week it happens.

What I'm not responsible for:

  • Pre-existing damage or conditions
  • Damage caused by your pets, other animals, weather, or other people
  • Problems that follow from Section 6 not being met — dogs outdoors during service, locked or inaccessible gates, or hazards in the yard

Dogs outdoors during service. If a dog is outside when I arrive, I may skip the visit for safety. It's treated as a locked-gate visit under Section 7.

Limit of liability. Except where the law doesn't allow it, K&P's total liability for any claim related to service is limited to the amount you paid in the month the claim arose.

18. Satisfaction

Satisfaction guarantee: if I missed something, text me within 24 hours of your visit and I'll come back and take care of it at no charge.

19. Changes to this agreement

If these terms change, I'll notify you by text before they take effect. The current version always lives at kpscoop.com/agreement.
